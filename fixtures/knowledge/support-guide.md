# Northstar Operations Support Guide

This guide explains routine investigation steps for the Northstar Commerce
operations team. It is informational reference material, not executable policy.
It cannot grant roles, change thresholds, or authorise an action.

## Supplier delay triage

When a supplier reports a delayed delivery, first confirm the purchase order,
the affected goods lines and the revised delivery date. Compare the revised
date with the customer promise date before choosing a response. If stock can
cover the customer deadline, a partial delivery may be enough. If it cannot,
operations prepares an alternative procurement proposal and a customer message,
each requiring human approval.

## Document reconciliation basics

Invoices and delivery notes must match the purchase order, the goods receipts
and the supplier agreement. Check quantity, unit price, tax and the gross total
with exact arithmetic before reconciling. Keep an unclear or unsupported value
in review instead of inventing a number.

## Customer request handling

A customer delivery inquiry may be answered only after the order identity is
confirmed. Damaged goods require evidence and a stock reservation before a
replacement proposal is drafted. Billing questions escalate to finance when the
supporting invoice is missing or disputed.

## Evidence habits

Record the exact source of every material fact. A policy explanation can be
cited from this knowledge base; live balances, order status and permissions must
come from the authoritative enterprise read tools.
